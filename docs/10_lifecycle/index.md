# Lifecycle Processes

## Purpose

This section contains lifecycle-stage process documents.

These documents describe how work progresses between lifecycle states and
baselines defined by `docs/00_governance/lifecycle_model.md`.

They complement governance documents by explaining expected workflow, review,
and readiness activities for specific lifecycle transitions.

## Current Coverage

- 1. [B0 Requirement Capture Process](t0_b0/b0_requirement_capture_process.md) -
  capture and structure pre-baseline requirement content for the initial
  `SSS-SYS.md` baseline.
- 1. [B0 Readiness Review](t0_b0/b0_readiness_review.md) - review the baseline
  candidate and record the final `Go` / `Hold` decision.
- 1. [T0 to B0 to SSS-SYS Mapping](t0_b0/t0_b0_to_sss_sys_mapping.md) - map
  T0-B0 outputs into the `SSS-SYS.md` template structure.

## Usage

- Read governance documents first.
- Use lifecycle process documents to understand transition-specific activities.
- Keep templates, examples, and checklists that are reusable across documents in
  a separate templates area when introduced.
